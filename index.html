    FAQ

    Good questions

    Can't find your answer? Ask Emma during the free class.

    Is the free class really free?

    Yes. No card needed. At the end, Emma explains the paid classes and course, and you decide if you want to continue.

    What level do I need?

    Classes suit lower-intermediate to advanced students. If you can read this page, you're ready.

    Do I need to turn on my camera?

    No. Many students keep the camera off for the first class. You can speak, type in the chat, or just listen.

    What if I miss the class?

    Everyone who signs up gets the recording by email, so you can watch it later.

    How do I pay for a class?

    Pick a class or plan and pay securely by card, UPI or PayPal. Prices are in US dollars and your bank converts them.

    Can I get a refund?

    Yes. Cancel at least 24 hours before a class for a full refund. If your first course class isn't right for you, tell Emma that day for a full refund.
