What we cover

Each week has one speaking class and one practice class.

  1. Week 1

    Speaking without translating

    Answer in English first, then improve.

  2. Week 2

    Small talk

    Openers, follow-ups and polite ways to leave.

  3. Week 3

    Sounds that matter

    Stress, rhythm and the vowels people mix up.

  4. Week 4

    Telling stories

    Past tenses that sound natural when you speak.

  5. Week 5

    Opinions and reasons

    Agree, disagree and explain without sounding rude.

  6. Week 6

  7. Week 7

    Interviews

    The ten questions you'll almost always get.

  8. Week 8

    Putting it together

    A short talk to the group, with feedback.

FAQ

About the course

When does the course start?

A new group starts every week. You choose your start date at checkout.

What if I miss a class?

Watch the recording and send your voice note as usual. You can also join the same class with another group that week if there's a seat.

How much time do I need each week?

Two hours of live classes and about 15 minutes a day with the workbook.

Is the workbook printed?

It's a PDF you can print. You get it as soon as you book.

Can I pay in my own currency?

Prices are in US dollars. Your card, UPI or PayPal converts the amount automatically.
