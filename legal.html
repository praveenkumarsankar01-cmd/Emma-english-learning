Privacy, terms and refunds

Learn English with Emma runs small live English classes online. This page explains how we use your details, the rules for booking, and how refunds work. Questions: XXXX@XXXX.com.

Privacy

When you sign up for the free class or book a paid class, we collect your name, email, English level and, if you add it, your WhatsApp number and country.

  • We use your details to send your Zoom link, reminders, class notes and receipts.
  • After the free class we send a few emails about the paid classes. You can unsubscribe from any email.
  • Payments are handled by our payment provider. We never see or store your card details.
  • Classes may be recorded for students in that class. Your camera is always optional.
  • We don't sell your details. To see or delete what we hold about you, email us.

Terms

  • Prices are in US dollars and include any fees we charge. Your bank may add a conversion fee.
  • A single class is for one person and one class. A 4-class pack must be used within 8 weeks of purchase.
  • The 8-week course runs with a fixed group. Recordings stay available until the course ends.
  • Please be kind in class. We may remove anyone who is abusive to other students.
  • Class materials, including the Speaking Guide, are for your own use only.

Refunds

  • Cancel a single class at least 24 hours before it starts for a full refund.
  • Unused classes in a 4-class pack can be refunded within 14 days of purchase.
  • First-class guarantee for the course: if your first class isn't right for you, tell Emma that day for a full refund.
  • Refunds go back to the payment method you used, usually within 5 to 10 working days.
